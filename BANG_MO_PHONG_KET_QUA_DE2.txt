I. QUY UOC CAC NGOAI VI TREN BOARD ALTERA DE2:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CLOCK_50 (PIN_N2)    : Nguon xung nhip thach anh 50MHz cua board DE2.
2. KEY[1]    (PIN_N23)   : Nut nhan RESET he thong (Nhan = Reset ve 0, Nha = Chay).
3. KEY[0]    (PIN_G26)   : Nut nhan BAM TUNG XUNG CLOCK (Single-Step) giup xem tung chu ky.
4. SW[17]    (PIN_V2)    : Cong tac che do Clock (Gat xuong 0 = Bam tay KEY[0]; Gat len 1 = Tu dong chay).
5. SW[16]    (PIN_V1)    : Cong tac toc do tu dong (Gat xuong 0 = Chay cham ~1.5Hz; Gat len 1 = Nhanh 50MHz).
6. SW[4:0]               : 5 cong tac gat de CHON SO HIEU THANH GHI (x0 - x31) muon xem gia tri.
7. HEX7-HEX6             : 2 LED 7 doan ben trai hien thi CON TRO LENH PC (Hex 8-bit thap: 00 -> 60).
8. HEX5-HEX4             : 2 LED 7 doan o giua hien thi SO HIEU THANH GHI dang chon qua SW[4:0] (00 -> 1F).
9. HEX3-HEX0             : 4 LED 7 doan ben phai hien thi GIA TRI 16-BIT cua thanh ghi dang chon (dang Hex).
10. LEDG[0]  (PIN_AE22)  : Co Zero cua ALU (Sang khi ket qua ALU bang 0).
11. LEDG[1]  (PIN_AF22)  : Co STALL (SANG CHOI khi xay ra xung dot phu thuoc du lieu Load-Use).
12. LEDG[2]  (PIN_W19)   : Co BRANCH/JUMP (SANG khi co re nhanh thanh cong hoac lenh nhay).
13. LEDG[3]  (PIN_V18)   : Den chop tat theo nhip xung clock CPU dang dap.
14. LEDR[15:0]           : 16 Den LED do hien thi du lieu ghi Write-Back (wb_write_data).
15. LEDR[16] (PIN_AE12)  : Den bao HALT (Sang khi CPU hoan tat va dung o vong lap cuoi 0x60).
16. LEDR[17] (PIN_AD12)  : Den bao THANH CONG (Sang khi x18 = 100, xac nhan toan bo chuong trinh test PASSED).

IV. HUONG DAN BIEU DIEN TREN BOARD DE2 KHI CHAM DO AN:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Nhan KEY[1] de Reset: HEX7-6 hien 00, tat ca thanh ghi ve 0.
2. Gat SW[17] xuong muc 0 de chon che do bam tay tung buoc.
3. Moi lan nhan KEY[0], PC nhay 04 -> 08 -> 0C -> 10...
4. Khi den buoc 12 (PC = 2C): Nhan KEY[0] tiep se thay PC van dung yen o 2C va den xanh LEDG[1] (STALL) sang bung len.
   Giai thich voi giang vien: Day la co che phat hien xung dot phu thuoc du lieu Load-Use, CPU chen 1 chu ky NOP bong bong.
5. Khi den vong lap Fibonacci (PC = 48 -> 58): Nhan KEY[0] se thay PC nhay lui 5 lan, den xanh LEDG[2] (BRANCH) chop sang
   xac nhan co che huy lenh sai (Flush) va re nhanh thanh cong.
6. Khi den dia chi 60: Den do LEDR[17] sang ruc, xac nhan toan bo 100% bai test deu PASSED!
